Keep `pendingArgs`, `timer`, `invokedLeading` and `callsSinceLeading`. Every call must store its arguments as `pendingArgs` before deciding whether it is the leading call, and must count itself towards `callsSinceLeading` even when it happens re-entrantly inside the leading invocation (mark the leading invocation as started before calling `fn`). The trailing edge fires only when a call happened after the leading invocation, and uses the latest `pendingArgs`. `cancel` clears the timer and also `pendingArgs` and the counters. `flush` invokes only when a trailing call is actually pending, clears the timer, then resets the burst state.
