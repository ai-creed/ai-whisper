Define `type Formatter = (report: Report) => string` in `registry.ts` with a module-level `Map`; `registerFormatter` throws on an existing name, `getFormatter` throws `unknown format <name>` for a missing one, and `listFormats` returns the keys in insertion order. Move the three switch branches verbatim into functions in `formatters/index.ts` and register them at import. `markdown.ts` exports its formatter, and `formatters/index.ts` registers it after the other three (ES imports are hoisted, so a module that registers itself on import would register before them and change the order). `format.ts` imports `./formatters/index.ts` for side effects and calls `getFormatter(format)(report)`.
